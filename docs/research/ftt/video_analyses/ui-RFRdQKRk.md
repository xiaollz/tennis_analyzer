## 核心命题

**Coil ≠ 转成侧身。** Coil = 把胸廓拧到一个**不跟着转的髋**上面，让后侧臀肌像弹簧一样上弦。

> [00:07] "Coiling means more than just turning sideways... here I am not coiled, I'm just turned sideways. Here I'm actually **coiled into this hip**."

## 关键机制（按时间戳）

### 1. Unit 的重新定义 [00:35]

> "When we say unit turn, we are talking about your whole thorax... elbow, shoulder, chest, both hands, the racket. That's the unit. **The unit doesn't include your hips.**"

Unit turn 转的是胸廓整体；**髋不在 unit 里**。如果髋跟着肩一起往后转 → "I create no tension, which can then unwind through the swing"（没有任何可以在前挥中释放的张力）。

### 2. 装载机制 = 反向拧，不是下压 [00:35–01:03]

> "My pelvis is going to **drive back towards you / towards the camera** as I coil. Take your hitting hip, **rotate it out, drive this point forward**, and then perform your unit turn. And now **my back glute is loaded**, and the second I **stop** driving my shoulders back, **it pops and unwinds me**."

- 骨盆向前顶（击球侧髋外旋顶出）作为**锚**，胸廓向后拧 → 后臀上弦。
- 释放是**被动**的：停止向后拧的那一瞬间，臀"pop"自动把身体解旋。不是主动用臀发力。

### 3. 骨盆 = 对地锚点 [02:04–02:33]

> "The pelvis is going back with my body **versus holding the pelvis so that I load against it**... this point is **anchoring through the ground**."

对比演示：髋肩同去同回（back together, forward together）= 只能靠往上挥补偿，球软；counter-rotate = 同样力气球质完全不同。

### 4. Coil 生效的副作用 [03:00–03:28]

> "Once you start actually getting power from your trunk, you're going to have to **throw the racket more overhand** to keep the ball [in]... if I use that underhand swing with the coiled version, goes 100 ft out."

Coil 上弦后力量变大，必须更 overhand / 更向下打才能保持落点——**球突然变长不是退步，是 coil 生效的信号**。

### 5. 四个单脚 counter-rotation 平衡模式 [03:28–05:26]

单脚站立挥拍（正手前脚/后脚、反手前脚/后脚共 4 种）：准备时髋向前送、挥拍时髋向后收，与肩等量反向（equal and opposite）。

> [04:31] "This is so far from what you're used to doing... your brain's going to **want to discover** the counter-rotational pattern... because it is so obvious just how much better the swing works." — 物理约束自组织，不靠口令。
> [04:58] "Stretch fire, stretch fire... hips and the shoulders playing equal and opposite roles at the trunk. We are always **corkscrewing one side against the other**."

### 6. 平衡与变向 [05:26–08:08]

- "You'll **never keep your balance at speed** if you don't counter-rotate." 高速下不反拧必失衡。
- [06:50] "I just have this **hip lever** that I can use to control where the ball ends up." 髋杠杆同时给功率和落点控制。
- 越进攻越要会**前脚 coil**（leaning in 时后脚 coil 别扭）。

### 7. 药球 drill（本视频版本）[08:08–09:32]

> "You can practice these counter-rotational balance patterns with a medicine ball. This is off of your front foot... your goal is just **see how many reps you can get through without having to put your foot down**."
> "**We're NOT trying to throw the med ball, we're not trying to use our hands**... It's just an **underhand release** of the med ball and you're **accelerating your hitting shoulder** towards your partner or the wall. Twist, twist, and just let the ball go."

要点：
- 单脚站（4 种模式同上），拧-拧-松手。
- **手不参与扔**，只是松开释放；加速的是**击球侧肩**。
- 进阶：保持平衡的前提下逐步加转速。

### 8. 走步捡拍 drill [10:00–10:58]

走向球 + 准备时抬起一只脚："I'm just trying to hit my **last step balanced and coiled**." 最后一步 = 制造锚的那一步（对应 [02:04] "that last step is a desire to hit this position that creates that anchor for my twist"）。

## 与 FTT 官网文章交叉验证（The Hip-Shoulder Separation Catastrophe）

https://faulttoleranttennis.com/the-hip-shoulder-separation-catastrophe/

- 实测：职业前挥开始时骨盆确实先于躯干约 **25ms**——但 25ms **不可感知**（"you'd hear only a single beep"）。
- **不要**追求"先转髋"的体感："In the version of the swing where you can, in fact, feel the hips moving first... the swing is almost certainly broken."
- 正确体感 = **腹部/筋膜张力，时序上是同时**："Tension through the abdomen while twisting. Timing wise, simultaneous."
- 臀肌体感出现在**旋转中段**（类比硬拉 sticking point），不是启动瞬间。
- 作者更推荐反直觉 cue "trunk → hips"——因为它产生的体感恰好造出 hips → trunk 的物理现实。与 7/17 上午 TopCourt 查证（躯干指挥、腿部倍增）完全一致。
