## Core Claim

Tomaz Mencinger frames the forehand as a changing blend of:

| Component | Function | Risk if misunderstood |
|---|---|---|
| **Throw / Release** | Let the biomechanical chain accelerate the racket with a loose arm. | If done without geometry/control, ball direction and height become unstable. |
| **Push / Drive / Control** | Add a small guiding component so the racket travels through the ball and the face stays aligned. | If it becomes dominant, the arm/shoulder tighten and the chain stops. |

The important update is that **Push is not automatically wrong**. A controlled receiving/blocking shot, return of serve, or emergency ball may contain a high Push/control fraction. The failure is **dominant muscular pushing** that replaces chain release.

## Push, Through, Pulling, Throwing

| Term | Working definition in this project | Healthy version | Failure version |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Throwing** | A release mode: proximal-to-distal chain lets the racket accelerate late. | Loose distal arm, body rotation and segmental timing produce racket speed. | "Throw with the dominant hand" only: hand/forearm tries to launch the racket alone. |
| **Through** | An external racket-path result: the racket continues through the hitting zone before the finish. | Visible forward extension after contact, not immediate wrap or lift. | Trying to manufacture through by dragging the hand/arm linearly. |
| **Push** | A control/drive component that can stabilize direction and face angle. | Small guiding fraction layered after/with release; useful on returns and controlled drives. | Dominant shoulder/arm/chest push, short finish, stiff racket-face steering. |
| **Pulling** | Ambiguous word; must be disambiguated. | Body rotation "pulls" the relaxed arm as a consequence. | Hand/elbow/shoulder actively drags the racket toward/through the ball. |

Project shorthand:

```text
Throw/Release = energy mode
Through       = visible path through contact
Push          = control/drive fraction
Pulling Trap  = active dragging that blocks the chain
```

Therefore:

- **Push and Pulling are not the same thing.** Push can be a necessary control fraction; Pulling Trap is active dragging.
- **Through and Push are not the same thing.** Through is what the racket does through the hitting zone; Push is one possible internal intention that may or may not create it.
- **Throwing does not mean zero control.** Feel Tennis explicitly treats good forehands as a blend, not 100% uncontrolled release.

| Source | Existing project node | Relationship |

| **FTT** Out / Up / Through | Three-vector swing path | Through is a path vector, not a license to use hand/arm dragging. |
| **FTT** Press Slot / chest engagement | HSA interface | Gives the anatomical basis, but must obey the 5/12 rule: chest is a transmission interface, not the active engine cue. |

The three coaches converge on one principle:

> The racket must travel through the ball, but the player should not solve that by actively dragging the racket with hand, elbow, shoulder, or chest.

They differ in teaching language:

- FTT gives the **path geometry**: Out / Up / Through.
- TPA gives the **coordination-chain warning**: do not pull; release like a throw.
- Feel Tennis gives the **shot-selection ratio**: throw/release plus a variable push/control fraction.

## VLM Diagnostic Candidates

These are candidates for future VLM/video review, not settled user diagnoses:

| Candidate tag | Visual signs |
|---|---|
| `dominant_push_control` | Short/stiff swing, shoulder/arm carries racket forward as a unit, abrupt finish, little late racket acceleration. |
| `pulling_trap_hand_or_elbow` | Hand/elbow visibly moves first toward the ball, forearm/wrist stiffens before trunk/hip transfer completes. |
| `healthy_throw_through_blend` | Body chain initiates, arm stays loose, racket accelerates late, then continues through the hitting zone before natural finish. |
| `through_missing_wrap_early` | Contact is followed by immediate upward/inward wrap with little forward extension. |

Important: these are **visual hypotheses**. VLM should report geometry and timing evidence, then leave final training conclusions to court data.
