## 1. 解决的问题
很多人「把球推过去」（push through the shot）——手臂先动、身体还没转，力没从下往上传。

## 2. 根因
手臂抢先：学员 Nitsan「drops the arm … then turns and hits the ball」@1:47——手先动，转体在后。光喊「手臂放松」不够，有人放松不了，太松球也乱飞 @1:22–1:31。

## 3. 顺序（髋先、手臂后）
- 链条简化：「the kinetic chain to simplify it is legs, hips …」@0:18
- 「he has to delay the arm … make sure the hips go first and the arm second」@1:56–2:00
- 「it's the hips turn and then it's the arms turn to move」@2:23–2:26
- 「it's the order in which you do it, not the fact that you do nothing」@3:23
- 「I'm just trying to turn my hips before my arm」@3:33；「the faster I do that the harder I can hit it … the arm is going to come a little more delayed, but that's the sequence」@4:03–4:13
- 收尾口令：「hips first, arm second」@4:25

## 4. 「不情愿 / 被动的手臂」体感
- 「when a ball comes you just do nothing with the arm」@2:03
- 关键改写：「it's not that I do nothing, it's that there's something else to do and it's not the arm's turn yet」@2:09–2:15 ← 这就是「手臂不情愿」：不是硬压住手，而是**注意力被髋占满，手还没轮到**。
- 高尔夫类比：上杆顶点「just relax and let the club drop … it's that you're doing something else」@2:35–2:51
- 「you want a slow enough take back that you can fire something else before the arms」@2:54
