## A. 6ak4ZScH_gc
**解决的问题**：骨盆在前挥里到底往前转还是往后？为什么「把右髋送进球里」这句口令常常害人。
**根因链**：髋、肩之间是一条斜向筋膜线（外斜肌等，「corkscrew」）→ 力来自这条线被拉长 → 「送髋」的意图让人髋肩一起走（无拉伸）→ 没张力 → 力传不上去。
**多角度讲解 / 原话**
1. 同一股拉力两头用：「the same tension that flings your shoulder forward, flings your hip backwards.」@2:38
2. 骨盆先往前是为了多拉一点：「pull … on that front anchor just a little bit more before I let it fling forwards.」@3:26；「the pelvis does accelerate forward before the shoulders. So, that is true.」@7:28
3. 口令的坑：「they send … back together, front together like this」@5:05；「If I tension the shoulder anchor, now the hip can barely move through the shot.」@5:40
4. 髋通常不倒退，只是减速：「It's not actually going to reverse direction on many shots.」@7:56；「it's just going to slow down.」@7:52
5. 体感：「the moment that you can feel for is a moment where the pelvis is being yanked backwards and the shoulders are being yanked forward.」@5:51
6. 感知原理：「we don't really perceive motion. We perceive tension and thereby acceleration.」@9:55
7. 意图：「drive your shoulders forward, hips back」@3:50（意图口令，不是动作描述）
8. 例外：「If you are a player and you think about firing your hip and you hit way harder and it's in control, probably it's working for you.」@9:41
**观察点**：髋先于肩往前（正后方可见，OBS-25）；髋在肩超越时减速（OBS-71，正后方实测 unclear）；「被往后拽」= 体感 C 层。腿加载、45° 方向、手臂何时被动：本期未讲。
